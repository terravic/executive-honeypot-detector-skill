# Defensive Countermeasures and Escalation Protocols

This manual details defensive actions corresponding to the three threat classifications produced by the Bayesian Threat Matrix. All protocols prioritize executive safety, reputational preservation, legal privilege, and containment of corporate exposure.

---

## 1. Tier 1: Benign / Organic Relationship

When the assessment yields high confidence in benign organic dynamics:

- **Reassurance**: Communicate clearly that observed patterns align with ordinary interpersonal behavior.
- **Standard Compartmentalization**: Maintain baseline corporate hygiene—do not discuss unannounced material non-public information (MNPI), board deliberations, or classified technical details outside authorized corporate channels.
- **Endpoint Discipline**: Maintain screen locks, hardware MFA, and corporate device separation as standard practice for all personal contacts.

---

## 2. Tier 2: Commercial Fraud / Romance Scam

When indicators center on opaque wealth claims, rapid emotional attachment, and monetary extraction without corporate IP targeting:

1. **Immediate Financial Firewall**: Halt all wire transfers, bridge loans, cryptocurrency platform deposits, escrow arrangements, or luxury liquid gifts immediately.
2. **Freeze Account and Identity Exposure**: If personal banking details, passport scans, or intimate communications were shared, notify private wealth advisors and legal counsel promptly to institute fraud alerts and credit monitoring.
3. **Licensed Independent Verification**: Before maintaining contact, obtain objective verification of employment, corporate filings, and identity through vetted executive protection or licensed investigative counsel—never rely on documents or third-party references supplied by the partner.
4. **Evidence Preservation and Calm Disengagement**: Preserve message records, payment requests, and transaction identifiers securely before ending communication quietly to reduce retaliation or extortion escalation.

---

## 3. Tier 3: Advanced Counterintelligence Threat / Honeypot

Premature confrontation of a trained intelligence asset or corporate espionage operative introduces severe operational risk: it alerts handler networks, prompts rapid asset exfiltration, destroys forensic attribution opportunities, and may trigger retaliatory leverage (kompromat).

### Phase A: Immediate Behavioral Continuity
- Maintain ordinary conversational warmth and predictable daily routines.
- Do not accuse the partner, ask hostile verification questions, or hint that corporate security has been notified.
- Stop volunteering genuine strategic, technical, legal, or personnel information immediately; steer discussions smoothly to neutral personal subjects.

### Phase B: Passive Information Control (Canary Protocol)
- Never deploy a Canary test unilaterally if regulated corporate disclosures or securities compliance could be affected; coordinate first with the Chief Security Officer (CSO) and Corporate General Counsel.
- A valid Canary detail must satisfy four design rules:
  1. **Zero Real Harm**: Contains no authentic proprietary IP, MNPI, or personnel secrets.
  2. **High Uniqueness**: Uses a distinctive synthetic project label, non-existent administrative date, or harmless mock venue mentioned to no other person.
  3. **Casual Delivery**: Introduced naturally in response to a partner probe without theatrical emphasis.
  4. **Measurable Resurfacing**: Monitored strictly passively via partner follow-up questions, external competitor feedback, or security team threat intelligence.

### Phase C: Silent Technical Isolation and MDM Audit
- Do not wipe or factory-reset potentially compromised devices yourself, as doing so destroys forensic telemetry needed by corporate incident responders.
- Deliver corporate smartphones, laptops, tablets, and hardware authentication keys discreetly to the corporate security operations team for offline Mobile Device Management (MDM) and endpoint inspection.
- From a confirmed clean corporate terminal, silently rotate corporate passwords, VPN keys, email app tokens, and personal password manager master credentials.
- Cease using any gifted charging cables, power banks, USB drives, or smart electronics; isolate them physically for security inspection without discarding them openly in front of the partner.

### Phase D: Privileged Institutional Escalation
- Initiate a direct, private briefing with the **Chief Security Officer (CSO)** and **Corporate General Counsel** under explicit attorney-client privilege.
- Do not transmit diagnostic summaries over standard corporate email or chat systems that could be compromised or broadly discoverable.
- Allow General Counsel and corporate counterintelligence leadership to determine whether external liaison with relevant federal counterintelligence authorities is warranted.

---

## 4. Hard System Boundaries

Under no circumstances shall this skill recommend or assist with:
- Covert audio/video recording, GPS tracking, or unlawful surveillance of any individual.
- Unauthorized access to the partner's personal phone, email, cloud accounts, or private networks.
- Vigilante intimidation, counter-extortion, or retaliatory harassment.
